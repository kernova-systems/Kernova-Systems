Privacy Policy

Last updated — September 2026

Kernova Systems ("Kernova", "we", "us", or "our") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect when you use this website and how we use it.

Information We Collect

You can browse this website without providing personal information.

If you submit an enquiry through our contact form, we may collect your name, email address, company name, and the information included in your enquiry. We collect this information only to understand your enquiry and respond to you.

How Your Information Is Processed

Contact form submissions are sent to Kernova Systems by email using Resend. We do not operate a CRM, customer dashboard, advertising database, or marketing database through this website.

Resend may process and temporarily retain email and related log data as part of providing its email delivery service. Resend currently states that email and log data is retained for 30 days on its Free, Pro and Scale plans.

Your enquiry may also remain in our email account for as long as reasonably necessary to respond to and manage the enquiry.

Hosting

This website is hosted using Cloudflare. Cloudflare may process technical information and use strictly necessary cookies or similar technologies to provide security, performance, and network services.

Fonts

The fonts used on this website are self-hosted. The website does not load its fonts directly from Google Fonts servers.

How We Use Information

Information submitted through the website may be used to respond to enquiries, discuss requested services, communicate about potential projects, and provide information requested by you.

We do not sell your personal information.

Data Retention

We keep enquiry information only for as long as reasonably necessary for communication, project discussions, legal obligations, or legitimate business records. You may contact us to ask about personal information we hold about you.

Your Rights

Where applicable, you may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information, or raise a complaint about its processing. For privacy-related requests, contact us using the email address provided on this website.

Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy when our website, services, or data practices change. The latest version will always be published on this page.

Contact

For privacy enquiries, reach us at systemskernova@gmail.com.
